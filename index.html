What do you notice about the last column? Can you write a rule to find ∠a from the fold angle?

Rule: ∠a = 90° − 2 × fold angle
Why? The corner at D is 90°. Folding does not change the size of an angle, so the fold angle is copied on the other side of the fold line. The 90° corner is then made up of ∠a and two equal fold angles.
